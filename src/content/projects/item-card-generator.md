Physical cards for magic items and equipment make a real difference at the table — players can hold them, reference them without interrupting the flow, and hand them back when the item is gone. This generates print-ready cards from PF2e item data.

## What it does

- Takes item data (from the PDF Extractor or manual input)
- Renders print-formatted cards with name, traits, description, and usage
- Outputs to PDF or printable HTML
- Designed to fit standard card sleeves

## Why it exists

Handing a player a physical card when they find a magic item is one of those small GM touches that lands well. Doing it by hand for every item isn't practical — this makes it a two-second operation.
