If you've bought a lot from the Paizo store, downloading your library one PDF at a time is tedious. This tool authenticates with your Paizo account and batch-downloads your purchases to a local folder.

## What it does

- Authenticates with your Paizo account
- Lists all purchased digital content
- Downloads everything in bulk to a local directory
- Keeps your library organised and offline-accessible

## Why it exists

DRM-free PDFs you've paid for should be easy to back up and access locally. This makes that process a one-command operation instead of an afternoon of clicking.
